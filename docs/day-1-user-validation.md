# Day 1 — User validation and messaging refinement

Goal: verify in plain language that non-technical users immediately understand what Betonus does.

## 1) Interview setup (5 participants)

Recruit 5 people who are not security experts (operations managers, founders, office admins, support leads).

Target outcomes:
- Can they explain Betonus in their own words in under 15 seconds?
- Do they know what action to take next?
- Do they trust the product enough to request a demo?

## 2) 10-minute interview script

### Opening (1 minute)
- “You’ll look at a website for a security tool. There are no wrong answers.”

### First-impression test (2 minutes)
Show the page for 10 seconds, then ask:
1. “What do you think this product does?”
2. “Who is it for?”
3. “What would you click first?”

### Confidence test (3 minutes)
- “What part feels clear?”
- “What part feels too technical?”
- “If you had this problem at work, would this page feel helpful?”

### Objection test (2 minutes)
- “What concerns would stop you from booking a demo?”
- “What proof would make you trust this?”

### Closing (2 minutes)
- “Rate clarity from 1 to 5.”
- “What single sentence should we change first?”

## 3) Scoring rubric

Score each participant 1–5 for:
- Clarity
- Trust
- Actionability

Day 1 success criteria:
- Average clarity score >= 4.0
- At least 4/5 users can correctly describe the core value proposition.
- At least 4/5 users identify the primary CTA without hesitation.
